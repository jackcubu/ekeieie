Serendipity

/ˌser.ənˈdɪp.ə.ti/
noun

Sự tình cờ may mắn; tình cờ tìm thấy điều tốt đẹp.

Definition

The occurrence of finding something good or useful without looking for it.

Example

Finding this little bookstore was pure serendipity.
